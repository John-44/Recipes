## Time-jump convention

Every cooking time jump uses the same small amber clock icon and a 2-second dissolve/card:

- First line: **clock +{elapsed} | {process}**. Write numeric card durations in full, such as “+35 minutes” and “+2 hours”. “Clock” means the icon; do not print the word or curly brackets.
- Second line: the cumulative process time and the next check or state.
- Keep the same label visible over the resulting food state for at least 2 seconds after the card, giving at least 4 readable seconds in total before a new action starts.
- Keep the card in the same lower-left position, within the central 90% of the frame. Use warm-ivory text on a dark translucent panel; amber distinguishes the clock. Keep captions in a separate lower band.
- The 2-second card and 2-second state hold are included in the shot durations below, not added afterwards.
- “+15 minutes” across P22–P25 covers the whole demonstrated filling window while the case bakes. It does not add another 15 minutes after the mixing. Likewise the +5 minutes at P40 includes the shortened whipping demonstration.
- Shortening an unspecified repeated action, such as crimping or scooping, says **Repeated [action] shortened**. A known-duration active process says **Demonstration shortened — [actual duration]**. These are editorial labels, not extra waiting time.
- Overnight is retained as “Overnight” where that is the recipe's instruction. “Until firm” in the freezer alternative has no invented numeric duration.
- Conditional and make-ahead inserts say **Only if…** or **Alternative…**, then **Main path resumes** where appropriate. They do not advance the main-path clock.
