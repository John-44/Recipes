# 🍖 Coca-Cola Ham

A whole gammon joint simmered for hours in Coca-Cola with vegetables and spices, then skinned, coated in a glaze of black treacle, English mustard powder and demerara sugar, studded with cloves and roasted until sticky. The long simmer makes the ham tender and juicy, and the Coca-Cola gives it a gentle sweetness. The glaze is dark and sweet, with a kick of mustard and the warmth of cloves. Serve it hot in slices, or cold in sandwiches.

| Prep time | Cook time | Total time | Servings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 40 min | 6 hr 10 min | 7 hr 5 min | 16 |

## 📌 Before you start

- Buy a mild-cure gammon joint. A saltier one may need soaking for 8–12 hours first (see **Mild-cure gammon joint**).
- Buy Coca-Cola Original Taste, not Diet Coke or Coca-Cola Zero Sugar (see **Coca-Cola Original Taste**).

## 🔪 Equipment

- Pot holding at least 10 L, with a lid
- Ladle
- Probe thermometer
- Roasting tin

## 🥕 Ingredients

#### For the ham

- 1 mild-cure gammon joint (about 4 kg)
- 5 L Coca-Cola Original Taste (used in Steps 1 and 2)
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and roughly chopped
- 2 celery sticks, roughly chopped
- 2 onions, peeled
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tbsp black peppercorns

#### For the glaze

- 3 tbsp black treacle
- 4 tsp English mustard powder
- 4 tbsp demerara sugar
- About 150 whole cloves

## 🍳 Directions

#### Step 1: Fill the pot.

Put the gammon in the pot, skin side down, and pour in enough of the Coca-Cola to cover it completely. Keep the rest for Step 2.

Peel and roughly chop the carrots, roughly chop the celery and peel the onions. Add them to the pot with the cinnamon sticks, bay leaves and peppercorns.

> **Tip:** The more snugly the gammon fits in the pot, the less Coca-Cola it takes to cover it, so in a wide pot, 5 L may not be enough.

#### Step 2: Simmer the gammon.

Bring to boil over high heat, 30–45 minutes, then turn the heat down to medium-low, so it simmers. Put the lid on, and cook for 4 hours 45 minutes. For a joint of a different weight, cook it for 1 hour per kg, plus 45 minutes.

Check it every 30 minutes. Top it up with more of the Coca-Cola as needed, so the gammon always stays covered, and skim off any foam with the ladle, taking care not to lift out the vegetables and spices.

When the time is up, push the probe thermometer into the centre of the thickest part of the ham. It should read at least 70°C. If it doesn't, keep simmering, and check again every 15 minutes.

#### Step 3: Skin and score the ham.

Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan).

Carefully lift the ham out of the Coca-Cola, and put it on a chopping board, skin side up. Use a sharp knife to cut off the skin, leaving a layer of fat.

Score the fat with the knife in two directions, with the lines about 2 cm apart, so they cross to make diamond shapes. Take care not to cut into the meat.

#### Step 4: Massage in the glaze.

Lift the ham into the roasting tin. Mix the treacle, mustard powder and sugar together in a small bowl, until there are no lumps of mustard powder.

Carefully massage half the glaze into the scored fat and over the rest of the ham. Keep the other half for Step 6.

#### Step 5: Stud the ham with cloves.

Stud every point where the score lines cross with a clove.

#### Step 6: Roast the ham.

Once the oven is at temperature, roast the ham for 15 minutes. Then pour the rest of the glaze over it, and roast it for another 30 minutes, basting it halfway through (spooning the glaze and juices from the roasting tin back over the ham), until the glaze is sticky and bubbling.

#### Step 7: Serve the ham.

Take the ham out of the oven and leave it to rest for 10 minutes. Then carve it into slices about 5 mm thick, picking out the cloves as you go.

## 📝 Notes and substitutions

- **Mild-cure gammon joint:** It doesn't need soaking, but other gammon, such as from a butcher, can be much saltier. Fry a small slice for 2 minutes a side and taste it, and if it's very salty, soak the joint in cold water in the fridge for 8–12 hours before Step 1.
- **Coca-Cola Original Taste:** It must be normal Coca-Cola, not Diet Coke or Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, because some sweeteners turn bitter when cooked.
- **English mustard powder:** This recipe uses Colman's Mustard, sold in small yellow tins.

## 🫙 Storage and make-ahead

- 🧊 **Fridge:** Up to 2 days, wrapped in cling film or in an airtight container.
- ❄️ **Freezer:** Up to 3 months in a freezer bag. Thaw it in the fridge overnight, and eat it within 24 hours.

- **Country:** 🇺🇸 United States
